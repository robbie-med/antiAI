21 Journal article Holmgren: +1.81 RVU/wk (5.8%), $3,044/yr, +0.80 encounters, no denial rise
Holmgren AJ, Fenton CL, Thombley R, Soleimani H, Croci R, DeMasi O, Byron ME, Murray SG, Adler-Milstein JR, Yazdany J. "Ambient Artificial Intelligence Scribes and Physician Financial Productivity." *JAMA Netw Open* 2026;9(1):e2553233. doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.53233. PMID 41511775. Cohort study, UCSF, ~1.2 million ambulatory encounters, 1,565 physicians, January 2023 to April 2025; 698 adopters (44.6%). Adopters: +1.81 RVUs/week (5.8%), approximately $3,044 additional annual revenue per physician at 2025 Medicare rates; +0.80 encounters/week (2.8%); no difference in claim denial proportion. Author and institutional statements per UCSF DoC-IT release: the findings are initial evidence that AI scribes change how physicians code and bill, with the open question being whether gains reflect additional services or changed coding practice. Single health system; voluntary early adopters. Cited by CMS in the CY2027 PFS RFI (see ref. 25).
DOI 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.53233 PMID 41511775 📄 local: holmgren_fulltext.xml
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12789954/ https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.53233 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41511775/
