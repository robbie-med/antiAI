## Key verified content

### Companion-study findings it frames (Holmgren, ref 21)
| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Weekly RVUs | +5.8% |

| Weekly RVU gain | 1.8 RVUs ≈ $3,000/yr per physician (2025 Medicare rates) |

> "…scaled across hundreds/thousands of clinicians, could collectively offset subscription fees."

### The author's warning (directly supports the essay's thesis)
> "Health systems that view ambient AI scribes solely as revenue enablers may underestimate their strategic value and risk overlooking the human and organizational factors that drive sustained productivity."

### The downcoding echo (another source naming the arms race)
> "Recent downcoding policies by Aetna and Cigna may signal a 'coding arms race' that could offset these gains."

### Indirect ROI (what the revenue framing misses)
- Burnout reductions of 20–40%; each departing physician costs $500k–$1M.
- "The path to productivity should not reintroduce the very pressures these tools were designed to relieve."
